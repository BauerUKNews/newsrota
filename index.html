Fixed, cover & rotation rules

fixed positions, cover and weekly turns

Fixed rules

reserved positions on selected weekdays
Autofill reserves these positions first, across all default templates. Only holiday absence releases the person for replacement. Cover and rotations work around them. Rules apply when you fill a weekday page; saved weeks are not changed automatically.
Weekdays

Cover rules

Set a chain: the first person is who's off, each name after covers the gap left by the one before. The last person's slot is left blank. Trigger a rule by right-clicking that person's cell in the rota (Edit mode on).

Rotation rules

roles that pass between people each week
Pick a role, then list who rotates through it. Autofill gives it to whoever’s turn it is that week — skipping anyone on holiday.

Default templates

the outlines new weeks & autofill start from
Each template applies to weeks starting on or after its date — the one before it covers everything earlier. Leave the date empty for the baseline. From a template's date, untouched week pages show its structure (empty, ready to fill), so a team restructure is visible on the rota in advance. Weeks you've edited by hand always keep your edits.

Save reusable roles and names for Saturday and Sunday. Friday and Monday are used only on bank-holiday weekends. Dates, holidays, notes and cover status are not saved in a default. Christmas and other special rotas are managed separately.

Using News Rota

Old backups: Operations → More options → View an old backup opens an empty, read-only viewer. Choose an exported JSON backup, browse its saved weeks and search within a week. It never restores or saves to the live rota. Close the viewer to return; loaded files are discarded when it closes.

Gap check: in Operations, choose More options → Run gap check. Select a whole month or inclusive date range to list unfilled roles across published and unpublished weeks. Open rota takes you to the relevant cell; Edit mode is not required to run the check.

Staff access: enter the four-digit PIN supplied by Operations. Operations can set or change it in the Access tab; changing it signs out existing Staff sessions. Phones remain Staff-only.
Find your rota: click the large date heading to open the week calendar, or use the arrows to move between weeks. Search for a person to open their personal rota, or search for a role or section. Phone mode is Staff-only; use a desktop for Editor or Operations access and editing. On a phone, use the arrows to move between full weeks or tap the W/C heading to choose a week. Scroll down for Monday to Sunday. Everyone shows the team; My rota uses your saved name.

Phone navigation: the bottom bar opens Rota, Holidays, Offices and Booths. Summary calendars start with one month; use Year overview to see the available year, then tap a month or a day. Personal rotas start with Upcoming; All dates includes previous weeks.

Daily totals: Holiday names and counts include unpublished weeks. Office occupancy and booth usage show published weeks and weekends only. Click a published date to see who is working. Full rota pages are available after publication.

Editor / senior leadership: view all rota pages, including unpublished ones, Admin and Personnel. This profile is read-only, including parking. Editing: select Operations, enter the team password, then turn on Edit. Setup guides you through templates, staffing and rules. Check the status at the top before closing the page.

Parking: Operations can switch on Commutes by car in a person’s details. The Parking tab shows expected drivers per office, including unpublished weeks. Manchester shows the bay diagram when you select a date: the calendar is green when everyone fits, orange when the emergency corner is used, and flashing red when anyone is unable to park. Other offices turn red at seven and flash at eight. Tap a date for names. People on holiday are excluded.

Weekend defaults: in Operations Edit mode, open More options → Weekend defaults. Create a blank default or copy the weekend you are viewing, save it with a name, then preview and apply it to another weekend. Dates and Away entries stay in place; anyone on holiday is skipped.

Cell colours: right-click a cell while editing to pick a colour. This changes its appearance only. Choose Automatic colour to restore the usual freelance or cover shading.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls. Use arrow keys in a viewing cell to move through assignments; Enter opens that person's schedule. Escape closes a dialog.

Connection or saving problem: open the status indicator at the top for connection details. Keep the page open while changes are pending.
