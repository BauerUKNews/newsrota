Cover & rotation rules

who covers who, and who rotates
Set a chain: the first person is who's off, each name after covers the gap left by the one before. The last person's slot is left blank. Trigger a rule by right-clicking that person's cell in the rota (Edit mode on).

Rotation rules

roles that pass between people each week
Pick a role, then list who rotates through it. Autofill gives it to whoever’s turn it is that week — skipping anyone on holiday.

Default templates

the outlines new weeks & autofill start from
Each template applies to weeks starting on or after its date — the one before it covers everything earlier. Leave the date empty for the baseline. From a template's date, untouched week pages show its structure (empty, ready to fill), so a team restructure is visible on the rota in advance. Weeks you've edited by hand always keep your edits.
